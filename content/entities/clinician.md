A behavioral-health provider participating in the platform. The declared states are a proposed progression from first contact through sustained practice: discovered through selected belong to clinician supply, verified through availability-defined are the onboarding band, match-ready is the threshold for entering matching, and active through sustaining describe a practice that is running rather than merely eligible. This ladder is a hypothesis and is expected to change.
