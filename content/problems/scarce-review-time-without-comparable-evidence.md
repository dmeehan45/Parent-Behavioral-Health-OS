# What happens today

The model can source, attract, screen, and select clinicians, but it does not represent how limited review capacity should be allocated across prospects with different amounts and quality of evidence. It also does not say which missing information is worth collecting before expensive judgment is spent.

# Why it matters

Giving every prospect the same manual path makes reviewer capacity a throughput constraint. Moving judgment into cheap rules or automation without enough predictive evidence can instead remove strong prospects before the system learns what it needed to know.

# Open questions

- What minimum evidence makes a prospect worth deeper human review?
- Which signals can support automated qualification, and when should uncertainty route a prospect to a person?
- Which sourcing channels can provide comparable evidence before asking the clinician to do more work?
- When is gathering one more piece of information worth its reviewer and candidate burden?
