A family seeking appropriate behavioral-health support. The declared states are a proposed progression through family demand: seeking-support marks arrival, need-understood means the care need is described well enough to say what clinician it requires, and match-ready is the threshold for entering matching — the same threshold the clinician ladder uses from its side. States for a family in active care are not yet declared, because the model has not yet described care delivery from the family's side. This ladder is a hypothesis and is expected to change, and it deliberately does not say who within the family holds authority at each state — that is the open question `define-family-patient-caregiver-authority`.
