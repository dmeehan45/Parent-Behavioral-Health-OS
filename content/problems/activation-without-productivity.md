# What happens today

Onboarding ends when a clinician is match-ready: verified, configured, and
available. Everything after that point is passive. The clinician waits to be
matched, and how quickly a first caseload comes together depends on what demand
happens to arrive while they are near the front of the queue.

# Why it matters

Being eligible and being productive are not the same state, and the model
currently only tracks the first one. A clinician who clears every onboarding
step and then sits idle looks identical, in the machine, to one who is fully
booked.

# Open questions

- How long does the gap between match-ready and first session actually last?
- Is the delay caused by the volume of demand, the shape of it, or the way it is
  allocated?
- Does an idle first month change whether a clinician stays?
